Our Heritage & Philosophy

Designed for Quiet Confidence & Lasting Comfort

Founded in 2018 between Milan and Paris, Aurelia Atelier was born from a desire to slow down modern fashion. Rather than chasing fleeting micro-trends, our studio focuses on architectural silhouettes, neutral tonal harmony, and tactile natural fibers.

Every garment is responsibly produced in small family-owned European ateliers using certified organic silk, traceable Mongolian cashmere, and Japanese raw denim—ensuring effortless comfort from day to night.
